Download BASS HARMONIZER

Windows VST3 zip · x64 Mac VST3 zip · universal

Windows: copy the BASS HARMONIZER.vst3 folder (the whole VST3 bundle, not a single file) into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\. You may need to allow Administrator access. If your plugins live on another drive (for example D:), copy that folder into the location your DAW actually scans.
Mac: drop the .vst3 folder into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ or /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
Then restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins.
