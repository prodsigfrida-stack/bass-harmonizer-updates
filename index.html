BASS HARMONIZER

BASS HARMONIZER is a free bass pitch-harmonizer plugin by SIGFRIDA. Put it on a bass, 808, or low-end track and it adds a second voice at a pitch you choose — mix, harmonics, microshift, distortion, reverb, and vibrato sit on that harmony.

  • Pitch harmony in semitone steps and octaves, with a wet/dry mix
  • Harmonics, microshift width, distortion, reverb, and vibrato on the pitched voice
  • Stable Pitch mode for cleaner tracking, mono below 250 Hz, and autogain
  • Free insert effect — VST3 on Windows, VST3 and Audio Unit on Mac

Windows x64 VST3 · macOS universal VST3 + AU · Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Reaper, GarageBand

Windows VST3 zip · x64 Mac VST3 + AU zip · universal

Windows: copy the BASS HARMONIZER.vst3 folder (the whole VST3 bundle, not a single file) into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\. You may need to allow Administrator access. If your plugins live on another drive (for example D:), copy that folder into the location your DAW actually scans.
Mac (Ableton, FL, Reaper…): drop the .vst3 folder into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ or /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/. Logic / GarageBand: drop the .component folder into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ or /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
Then restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins.
